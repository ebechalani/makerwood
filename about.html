About us

A small workshop that turns ideas into wood

MakerWood cuts, engraves and carves personalized pieces to order, one at a time or by the hundred.

MakerWood started with a simple idea: the gifts people remember are the ones made for them. A name on a bauble, the date two people met, a grandmother's recipe title on a board. Our laser and CNC machines let us make those pieces well, and make each one different.

Everything on this website is cut to order. When you place an order, we prepare your design, check the spelling with you if needed, then cut, sand and finish the piece by hand before it is wrapped.

We also build STEAM kits: flat-packed models that children assemble themselves and then use to learn how levers, hydraulics and energy work. Schools and clubs can order them as class sets.

Questions we are often asked

How long does an order take?

Most pieces are made to order within the time shown on each product page, usually 2 to 7 working days. Custom orders depend on the design and quantity; we give you a date before we start.

Can I see the design before you cut it?

Yes. For personalized and custom pieces we can send you a preview of the layout first, so you can check names, dates and spelling before anything is cut.

Can you make something that is not on the website?

Very likely. Signs, logos, event décor, school projects and gifts in larger quantities are a big part of our work. Send us a custom request with as much detail as you can.

Do you take school, club or company orders?

Yes. STEAM kits come in classroom packs, and we make branded gifts, medals and name tags in quantity. Tell us the number you need and your deadline.

How do I look after a wooden piece?

Keep it dry and out of strong sun, and dust it with a soft cloth. Serving boards should be hand washed, dried upright and oiled now and then. Nothing we make should go in the dishwasher.

What if something arrives damaged or wrong?

Send us a photo within 48 hours of delivery and we will repair or remake it. Personalized pieces are made just for you, so we can't take them back for a change of mind.
